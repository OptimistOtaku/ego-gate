# Expanded GPU benchmark status

Kaggle version 2 completed on two Tesla T4 GPUs. All 870 runs passed the local completeness, uniqueness, exposure-budget, and selected-index integrity audit. Raw evidence is under `results/kaggle_gpu/benchmark`; summaries and 27 primary comparisons are under `results/offline_benchmark`.

The expanded results are integrated into the current 10-page IEEE manuscript and PDF. All 27 primary contrasts are reported in the accompanying CSV; the paper includes central-budget results, memory curves, paired intervals, costs, and limitations. No significant primary-comparison wins were found; ten contrasts show significant losses. Final author review and venue selection remain pending.
